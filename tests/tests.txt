# Tests

## Test 1 - Sign in and use Mongolian AI report [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Google authentication, direct first-time dispatcher access without invite, GET /api/me auto-enrollment, Туслахаа Voice AI reliable push-to-talk control with single recognition session and explicit reset, POST /api/ai-manager complete-answer generation with explicit conclusion, smaller chunked sequential POST /api/tts full-answer playback with retry and sentence-level browser fallback, extended daily report rendering, sequential POST /api/tts long-report playback, audio playback and stop controls
Description: Verifies a new Google user can sign in directly without an admin invite, receives dispatcher access, and can use the sample Mongolian AI report and voice controls.
Steps:
1. Sign in with a new Google account that has no prior invite or role and enable Туслахаа Voice AI on the dashboard
2. Enable “Сэрээх үг” on a supported Chrome/Edge browser and verify the button visibly changes to “✓ Туслахаа идэвхтэй”; say “Туслахаа”, ask a transport question, and verify the assistant stays busy until the complete server TTS answer finishes, then returns to listening. Also verify “🎤 Ярих” still works; then open "AI Өдрийн илтгэл", press "Жишээ илтгэл", and press "Монгол AI-аар уншуулах"
3. Press "Зогсоох"
Expected: After Google sign-in the app auto-enrolls the user as Dispatcher and opens the dashboard without requiring an invite. Supported Chrome/Edge browsers visibly activate wake mode as “✓ Туслахаа идэвхтэй”, recognize “Туслахаа”, keep recognition paused while AI/server TTS is busy, play the complete answer, and automatically return to wake listening afterward. Push-to-talk remains available. Unsupported or denied microphone states visibly deactivate wake mode and explain what to do. The daily report is detailed enough for a multi-minute briefing, long report audio is split into sequential TTS parts and all parts play in order; Stop ends playback.
